I am Ye-Chao Liu, Associate Researcher at Hainan University from October 2026. 
Previously, I held two postdoctoral positions, focusing on entanglement distribution in quantum networks and optimization-based entanglement detection. 
My doctoral research also provided me with a strong background in quantum characterization, verification, and validation (QCVV).

My current research focuses on:
1) Learning key properties of large-scale quantum systems
2) QCVV for multipartite logical quantum systems
3) Theoretical methods for high-accuracy quantum control

I welcome inquiries from students and researchers interested in collaboration.
